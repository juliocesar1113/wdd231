01

Site Name

Name Amond Beauty
Domain idea amondbeauty.com
Why this name

"Amond" evokes the almond — a natural, nourishing ingredient strongly associated with haircare and skincare. The name is short, elegant, and easy to remember, while "Beauty" anchors it clearly in the cosmetics space. Together the name signal natural ingredients and a polished, trustworthy brand — exactly the impression a small-batch bar product business should make.

02

Site Purpose

Amond Beauty is an e-commerce store focused on handcrafted shampoo and conditioner bars. The website serves as both a brand destination and a direct sales channel, giving customers a thoughtful shopping experience distinct from the existing Shopify storefront.

What the site provides

The site presents the full product catalog of shampoo and conditioner bars, communicates the brand's natural and sustainable values, and provides a simple path to purchase. A contact page allows customers to ask questions, request custom orders, or reach the owners directly. The home page establishes brand identity through imagery, short storytelling copy, and highlighted featured products.

04

Visitor Scenarios

These are questions a target customer — someone interested in natural, sustainable haircare — might ask when visiting the site.

  • What shampoo and conditioner bars do you have, and which one is right for my hair type?
  • How are your bars made, and what ingredients do you use?
  • I want to order in bulk for a gift — how do I contact the store owners?
  • Is there a conditioner bar that works well with the shampoo bar I already bought?

07

Wireframe

The wireframe at the top of this document (rendered as an SVG diagram) shows the home page layout for both mobile and desktop viewports. Key layout decisions are described below.

Mobile (≤ 480px): Single-column stack — nav with hamburger menu, full-width hero with text overlaid on image, three feature cards in a scrollable row, then featured product cards stacked vertically, and a compact footer.

Desktop / Tablet (≥ 768px): Two-column hero (text left, product image right), three-column feature cards side by side, four-column product grid for featured items, full horizontal nav bar, and a multi-column footer with links and social icons.

Navigation: All viewports show the Amond Beauty logo/wordmark on the left. Desktop shows inline links (Home · Catalog · Contact) plus a small CTA button. Mobile collapses these to a hamburger menu.

Page hierarchy: Home → Catalog (product list) → Contact. Each product card in the catalog links to its purchase/detail page.
